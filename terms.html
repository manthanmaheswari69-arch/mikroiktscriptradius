Tool purpose

RouterForge provides browser-based configuration drafting tools and educational material for RouterOS. You are responsible for validating generated commands, protecting credentials, complying with your agreements and laws, and applying changes to your own equipment.

No operational guarantee

Network changes can interrupt connectivity or reduce security when used with the wrong topology, interface names, address plan, RouterOS version, firewall order, or RADIUS configuration. Test in a safe environment, keep backups, and maintain a recovery path. The site does not guarantee that generated output is suitable for every network.

Credentials and third-party services

Do not share passwords, RADIUS secrets, private keys, or customer data unnecessarily. RouterOS, MikroTik documentation, RADIUS services, Google services, and other linked services are governed by their respective owners and terms.
